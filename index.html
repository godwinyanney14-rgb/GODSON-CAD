GODSON Aircraft CAD Design Studio

A browser-based aircraft design workspace: browse the reference library, open a model, inspect its assembly tree, type exact dimensions into the parameter fields, choose nose and wing planform styles, cut real window holes through the fuselage skin, and export genuine STEP (faceted B-rep) and STL files. Models are reference-based or approximate reconstructions from published dimensions — they are not manufacturer proprietary CAD data and are not airworthiness-validated.
